## 🚀 Projects

### QR Token Management & Validation App

An Android application personally developed for an institute to create, print, validate, and expire physical QR discount tokens. Authorized users can scan a token, validate its unique number against Firebase, and destroy it when used. Destroyed tokens are marked expired in the backend to prevent reuse.
